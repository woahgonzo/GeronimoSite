The First Annual Geronimo Short Film Festival

A short film competition built around teams, audience voting, and a winner-takes-all cash prize.

Date
May 6, 2026
Venue
West Charleston Library
City
Las Vegas, NV
Type
Film Festival

The First Annual Geronimo Short Film Festival gave local teams a chance to create, compete, and screen their work for an audience. The night centered on independent filmmaking, community participation, and a cash-prize audience vote.
